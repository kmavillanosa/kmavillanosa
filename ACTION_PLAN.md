## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Don't spam post - quality over quantity
2. ❌ Don't ignore analytics - data tells you what works
3. ❌ Don't forget to update - stale profiles hurt credibility
4. ❌ Don't skip engagement - build relationships, not just applications
5. ❌ Don't use generic messages - personalize your outreach

## 🎓 Pro Tips

1. **Keywords Matter:** Use "Full Stack Developer", "React Developer", "Remote Developer" in profiles
2. **Consistency:** Keep all profiles updated simultaneously
3. **Timing:** Post on LinkedIn Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM or 1-3 PM
4. **Engagement:** Comment thoughtfully on recruiter posts (don't just like)
5. **Content:** Share technical insights, not just "looking for work"
