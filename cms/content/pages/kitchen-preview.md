**Kitchen Preview** is an intuitive kitchen design visualization tool that helps you create stunning kitchen designs in minutes.

## Features

- **Predefined Themes**: Choose from professionally curated themes or create your own custom design
- **Custom Colors**: Select from a wide range of colors for countertops, cabinets, floors, and more
- **Real Textures**: Apply realistic textures and materials to see how your kitchen will look
- **Save & Export**: Save your designs and export them as PDFs to share with clients or contractors

Create your first kitchen design in seconds. No sign-up required.
